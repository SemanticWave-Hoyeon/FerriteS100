# Background coastline data

Natural Earth public-domain reference coastlines, not ENC data or navigational coverage.

- 1:110 million: existing `ne_110m_coastline.geojson`.
- 1:10 million: `ne_10m_coastline.geojson`, downloaded 2026-10-05 from
  https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_10m_coastline.geojson
- Source description: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-coastline/
- Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Here “10m” means a 1:10 million map scale, not ten metre accuracy.
The 1:10 million layer is selected at 30 or more physical pixels per degree
of longitude; wider views use the original 1:110 million layer.
Neither layer is a substitute for loaded S-101 coverage.
